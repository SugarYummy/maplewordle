# Maple Wordle changelog

## alpha v4.3 - 21 October 2026
- BGM Clip now has two modes, each with its own BGM every day:
- Normal (new): 7 guesses, and the clip always starts at the beginning. Each guess unlocks more: 1s, 5s, 10s, 30s, 60s, then the whole track. After your 3rd guess you get a hint (the boss's World, Gender, Region, Phases, HP or Entry Lv), and on your 6th you get its Entry Lv
- Extreme: the original BGM Clip (0.5s to 30s, and the clip can start anywhere)
- BGM Clip mesos: Normal pays 50 / 40 / 30 / 20 / 10 / 5 / 2, Extreme now pays more: 100 / 80 / 60 / 40 / 20 / 10
- Mesos: Boss Wordle and BGM Clip now only pay for your best mode each day, so you don't have to play every difficulty. Do better on another mode and you get the difference
- Archive days now pay 10% of the mesos (rounded up). Already finished some Archive days? Open them from the Archive to collect
- Weekends pay 2x mesos! Saturday and Sunday (Maple reset), on that day's puzzles. Watch for the falling leaves
- The sound button is now a ⚙️ Settings button: sound on/off, volume, and the background effect on/off

## alpha v4.2 - 20 October 2026
- New look: the whole site now uses the Rubik font, so it looks the same on every device
- Meso numbers are bright gold now
- Puzzle number, next-puzzle timer and win streak now sit together in one info bar
- Shop has been added! Cosmetics being added soon!

## alpha v4.1 - 20 October 2026
- Mesos! Finish today's puzzles to earn mesos. The fewer guesses you use, the more you get
- Your mesos show in the top-right corner of the game box. Click them to see how much each game pays and what you earned
- BGM Clip pays 3x mesos all week, October 20 to 26!
- A cosmetic shop is coming later, so save up your mesos!

## alpha v4.0 - 20 October 2026
- New game: Guess the Boss - BGM Clip. Listen to a boss fight's BGM and guess the boss in 6 tries
- You start with 0.5 seconds of the clip; each wrong guess or Skip unlocks more (1s, 2s, 5s, 10s, then 30s)
- The clip starts at the very beginning of the track or somewhere in the middle, and can be from any part of the fight: waiting room, phases, loot room
- Bonus round: when the game ends the whole track unlocks. Guess which part of the fight it plays in, just for fun
- Win on your first guess for a ⭐, get the bonus right too for a 💎 in the Archive
- BGM Clip has its own volume bar next to Play, separate from the sound button
- Share: a first-guess win shows just a ⭐ (a 💎 in BGM Clip if the bonus was right too)
- Added a hidden Easter egg :) Keep playing every day to potentially see it!
